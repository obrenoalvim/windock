## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Floating dock with presets (environments), each with its own apps, accent color and widgets.
- Schedule per preset (days and time ranges), with automatic switching.
- Global hotkeys: `Ctrl+Alt+Space` shows/hides the dock, `Ctrl+Alt+1..9` picks a preset.
- Widgets: clock, CPU, RAM and next scheduled switch.
- Running-app indicator, drag-and-drop to pin a program, right-click to remove.
- Tray icon with preset list, auto-switch toggle, start with Windows and presets folder shortcut.
- Four starter presets: Trabalho, Jogos, Estudos, Fim de semana.
